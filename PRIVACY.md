# Privacy Policy — Beluga

Beluga does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No account, no analytics, no tracking, no network access.
- The app uses the macOS Screen Recording permission to capture your screen locally so the CRT filter can be applied. Captured frames are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere.
- Your settings (slider values) are stored locally on your Mac.
- No third-party SDKs.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/alespoio/Beluga/issues
