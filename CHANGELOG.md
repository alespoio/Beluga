## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-09

First public release of Beluga.

- Real-time CRT television effect for your entire Mac screen: curved glass, scanlines, RGB phosphor mask, glow, chromatic aberration, vignette and brightness, with 9 adjustable effects.
- Menu bar app in English and Spanish, with multi-display support (mirrored, extended and AirPlay).
